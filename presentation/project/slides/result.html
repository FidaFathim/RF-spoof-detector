Against PGD, classifier confidence is backwards: attacks look more confident than genuine signals.

RF spoof detection · 12 · Bars: 1% = 8 px

This is the main positive result, after the feature fix. We count only attacks that already fooled the CNN, because those are the ones that matter. The CNN's confidence is blind to strong attacks: against PGD it is actually backwards, with an AUC near 0.01, meaning forged signals look more confident than genuine ones. The physics check catches 23 percent of PGD at 0.2 and 56 percent at 0.4, and 32 percent of UAP at 0.4. Whole-system F1 for strong PGD goes from 0.00 to between 0.33 and 0.62. But the spread across seeds is large; for PGD 0.4 it was 78, 17 and 74 percent by seed. At weak strengths confidence is as good or better, for example FGSM 0.1: 21 percent for confidence versus 15 percent for the check. The direction at epsilon 0.2 and above is consistent in all three seeds.
