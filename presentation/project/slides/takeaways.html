CONCLUSION

What we conclude

1

Against strong PGD and UAP attacks, a per-device physical check catches forgeries that CNN confidence cannot. Whole-system F1 goes from 0.00 to 0.33 to 0.62.

2

Not supported by our data: that the features work together, that the joint check beats I/Q phase alone, or any benefit against small attacks.

3

Results swing by seed and false rejections vary by receiver. A promising direction, not a finished defense.

Next: other target devices, an attacker who copies the hardware numbers, the GAN attack, and over-the-air tests.
