RESULTS SO FAR · LIMITATIONS

Limitations we state up front

  • One impersonated device only (14-10). Other targets are untested.
  • Attacks were fed straight into the classifier. Over-the-air was not tested.
  • An attacker who copies the target's hardware numbers would defeat the check. That attack, and the GAN attack, are not implemented.
  • Small attacks are not detected (the control test). Large ones (ε = 0.2 to 0.4) are noisy.
  • The benefit depends on the session split: PGD at ε = 0.4 was caught 78%, 17%, 74% across seeds.
  • False rejections vary by receiver, and calibration needs several devices in the same session.
