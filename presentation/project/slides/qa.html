Backup: likely questions

Isn't this just anomaly detection?

Yes, it is a one-class model per device. What is new is using it as an independent second check against adversarial examples, and testing it honestly.

Can an attacker beat it?

Yes. One who estimates and copies the target's hardware numbers would pass a per-device check. We did not test that attack.

Does it really detect the attack?

At ε = 0.1 it rejects attacks as often as random noise (about 16%). Only large attacks clearly stand out: PGD at ε = 0.4 is rejected 78% against 20% for noise.

Why three seeds?

One seed can mislead: PGD at ε = 0.4 was caught 78%, 17% and 74% in three runs. We report mean and spread.
