RF spoof detection · 15

Every defense has a cost. Adding the check raises the rate at which genuine signals are wrongly rejected, from 8.3 percent to 12.5 percent on the full seed 0 test split. It was calibrated to reject 5 percent on validation data, so the gap between 5 and 12.5 percent shows it does not transfer cleanly to new sessions. The receiver breakdown shows why: rejection is 18.5 percent for receiver 18-2 and 0.9 percent for receiver 7-14. That means the check partly learns the receiver and environment instead of only the transmitter. We report this as a limitation rather than hiding it.
