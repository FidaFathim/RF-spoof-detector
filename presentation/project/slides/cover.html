Network security project

Catching RF spoofing with a second, physics-based check

An independent hardware-plausibility gate added to a CNN that authenticates radio transmitters

WiSig dataset · 6 devices · 12 receivers · 3 random seeds

Team: Fida Fathim, Bharat Suresh

Opening line: radios can be recognised by tiny hardware flaws, and that recognition can be attacked. Our project adds a second, independent check so that fooling the neural network alone is not enough. Everything I show comes from real runs on the WiSig dataset, averaged over three random seeds, and I will be upfront about where it works and where it does not.
