RF spoof detection · 4

This review is based on the papers already collected in our project notes, so call it preliminary. WiSig is our dataset. FGSM, PGD and universal perturbations are the attacks we implemented. The GAN impersonation attack by Karunaratne and colleagues is planned but not implemented. The closest defense we found is Yang et al. 2024: it uses one physical feature, power-amplifier nonlinearity, and flags impersonation when two classifiers disagree. Ours differs because it is a separate check on hardware numbers, not a disagreement between classifiers. The 2026 HWE attack by Albousayri and Hamdaoui estimates and copies a target's hardware impairments, so it is the hardest threat to a per-device physical check, and we have not implemented it. A systematic literature search is still on our plan before we finalise the novelty claim.
