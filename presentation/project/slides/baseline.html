An important result before any defense: the classifier itself. In our early runs the CNN reached only 16.7 percent accuracy on sessions it had never seen, which is chance for six devices; it had learned the channel, not the transmitter. With a cosine learning-rate schedule and best-checkpoint saving, it now reaches 93.8 percent on held-out sessions, averaged over three seeds with a standard deviation of 2.1 points. The split is by session so this number is not inflated by leakage.
