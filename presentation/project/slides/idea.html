RF spoof detection · 7

This is the whole design on one slide. The CNN asks: does this signal sound like device D? The physics gate asks a different question: do the hardware flaws in this signal look like they came from a real radio? The gate measures four physical quantities, learns what genuine signals look like, and never sees an attack during training. We accept only if both agree, so an attacker has to fool two unrelated systems. The attacks we tested only optimise against the CNN.
